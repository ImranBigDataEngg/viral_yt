# TrendTide 60 — Channel setup kit

- **Name:** TrendTide 60
- **Handle proposal:** `@TrendTide60` (not reserved or verified; handle may already belong to another account)
- **Fallbacks to check:** `@TrendTideDaily60`, `@TrendTideExplained`, `@TodayOnTrendTide`
- **Tagline:** Today's biggest trends. Explained in 60 seconds.
- **Voice:** sharp, curious, conversational, clearly fact-checked and distinct from copied creators.
- **Audience:** English-speaking gaming / AI / technology / entertainment discovery viewers, starting in Canada and global English markets.

## About section — copy/paste

> What's trending, and why does it matter? TrendTide 60 turns the biggest stories in gaming, AI, technology, and internet culture into quick, original, easy-to-follow explainers. Independent commentary, fresh perspective, useful context, and sources when relevant. New Shorts and deeper dives every week. Subscribe for the story behind the trend.

## Brand design brief

Create a flat, high-contrast round symbol combining an upward trend graph and wave, no copied platform logos. Palette: dark midnight navy `#111827`, electric cyan `#22D3EE`, warm coral `#FB7185`, white. Use one simple bold modern sans font. Profile icon must work at 98px and banner must respect the device-safe center area. Generate original assets, check licensing for fonts/icons, and don't depict GTA or other games as official channel sponsors.

Short design: 1080×1920; keep key text away from right-edge platform controls and bottom captions; clean 2–5 word hook at top; narration with first-2-second hook; source credit in description; no misleading official branding.

## Five repeatable, value-adding formats

1. **What Actually Happened?** — one verified event, 3 facts and original perspective.
2. **Trend in 60 Seconds** — context, why it blew up, what's next.
3. **GTA 6 Myth vs Confirmed** — separate documented announcements from speculation.
4. **AI Tool Reality Check** — first-hand test and measured pros/cons.
5. **Why Everyone's Talking About It** — one independent original explain-it piece.
