Questions

Can I clip any YouTube video?

No. You can clip videos from channels you've verified with Google, Creative Commons videos, and files you have rights to. Reposting other people's videos gets channels struck and demonetised, so we don't allow it.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes for a one-hour video. You can watch the progress live and leave the page.

What is the clip score?

An estimate from the transcript: how strong the opening line is, how cleanly the clip flows, and how much payoff it carries. It helps you choose what to post first. It's not a guarantee of views.

Do you store my card?

No. Checkout and billing are hosted by Stripe.
