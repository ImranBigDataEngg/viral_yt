Questions

Can I clip any YouTube video?

No. We clip videos on channels you've verified through Google, videos the uploader marked Creative Commons, and files you have the rights to. Reposting other people's videos gets channels struck and demonetised, so we don't do it.

Live streams?

Paste the stream link. If it's still live, we start automatically as soon as the replay is available.
