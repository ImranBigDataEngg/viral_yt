Frequently asked questions

Can I clip any YouTube video?

You can clip videos from channels you've verified, Creative Commons videos, and files you have rights to. Reposting other people's videos gets channels struck and demonetised, so we don't allow it.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes for an hour of video. Progress updates live, so you can leave the page.

Will my clips be monetisable?

Clips of your own content are. For permitted third-party clips, use "Make original" to add your own take. We never disguise other people's content, because platforms detect and penalise that.

Which platforms can I post to?

YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (Business/Creator accounts), through each platform's official API. You can also download MP4s for anywhere else.

What is the clip score?

An estimate from the transcript of hook strength, flow and payoff. It helps you choose what to post first, but it doesn't guarantee views.

Do you store my card?

No. Checkout and billing are hosted by Stripe.
